CONTEXT FOR POLISHING "InkCloser" (paper.tex / references.bib)

These are author decisions and known facts that are NOT stated in the paper. Respect them: do not "fix" them, do not add text that contradicts them, and do not add text that reveals the ones marked CONFIDENTIAL.

A. DATA REPRESENTATION (CONFIDENTIAL)
1. The source images and all stored clean/corrupted images are binary (0/255). The author does not want the paper to call the data "binary" or "anti-aliased", because it could be read as a limitation. Do not add either word anywhere.
2. Exception, kept on purpose: Section 3 defines Y in {0,1}^{H x W}. Keep it exactly as it is, and do not change it to [0,1].
3. Neutral wording is intentional: "monochrome line-art images" (Dataset), and "since they are specks rather than paintable regions" (Metrics), not "anti-aliasing specks". Keep both.
4. Training pairs have soft edges because of bilinear augmentation, and that is why the hole indicator uses thresholds (X > 0.9, Y < 0.5). This is not explained in the paper and should stay unexplained.
5. The source images are already 896x896, so "Each image is resized to 896x896" is effectively a no-op. Keep the sentence as it is.

B. TRAINING LENGTH AND SCHEDULE
6. The reported model was trained for 5 epochs (11,250 steps). The paper states 5 epochs.
7. The cosine schedule was configured for 30 epochs (T_max = 30, eta_min = 1e-6) but training stopped after 5, so the learning rate barely decayed. The paper only says "decayed with a cosine annealing schedule". Do NOT add "down to 1e-6", the 30-epoch configuration, or any claim that the schedule completed. The author chose not to explain the truncated schedule.
8. The public training notebook still says epochs = 30. The author will update it later. Do not mention this in the paper.

C. MODEL SELECTION AND VALIDATION (do not add claims)
9. There is no separate validation split. During training, the test set was evaluated at the end of every epoch, for monitoring. The hole weight (lambda_h = 3 instead of 8) came from "preliminary experiments". Do NOT write that no test data was used for model selection or tuning of OUR model. (The paper says this only for the retrained Sasaki baseline, where it is true.)
10. Do not add a validation-split description or any early-stopping claim.

D. ABLATIONS
11. An ablation study now exists (Section "Ablation Study", Table tab:ablation; code in code/ablations, results in paper/v1/results_ablations). Every variant, including the full model, was trained for 1 epoch (2,250 steps) with the paper's settings; the w/o 28x28 attention variant was also trained for 1 epoch, but the paper does not tabulate that run (see 12). The paper says "one epoch instead of five". Do not mention the cosine schedule's configuration (see B.7), and do not compare step counts with the reported model beyond "five epochs".
12. At 1 epoch, removing the 28x28 bottleneck attention did NOT hurt; the variant without it was slightly but significantly better on gap closure (+1.9), bucket acc. (+1.3) and leak-free (+5.5). Author decision: Table tab:ablation leaves this row out, and the paper says that one epoch is not enough for this ablation. The evidence for that is stated in one sentence (bucket acc. and leak-free advantages reversed/disappeared at full length), and the full-length paragraph notes that the gap-closure advantage was "already" there at one epoch. Keep both: they are what keeps the omission honest. The full numbers are in code/ablations/README.md and paper/v1/results_ablations.
12b. A second comparison (Table tab:ablation_long, paragraph "Bottleneck attention under longer training"; results in paper/v1/results_ablations_9155) retrains only the full model and w/o 28x28 attention for the released model's real length, 9,155 steps (4 epochs + 155 steps), seed 0. The paper calls this "the training length of the reported model" / "the same number of iterations as the reported model" and gives no step or epoch count, so that it stays consistent with "five epochs" (B.6). Do not add 9,155, "four epochs" or any step count. Results: with attention, fill F1 +0.7 and bucket acc. +0.2 (significant), leak-free n.s.; without attention, gap closure still +3.6 (larger than at 1 epoch). Both retrained models are below the reported model on gap closure (59.1 / 62.6 vs 70.5); the paper reports this and attributes it, hedged, to run-to-run variance (the reported model was trained unseeded, which the paper does not say). Keep both the negative gap-closure result and the variance caveat.
13. The ablations support the gap Dice term, the gated convolutions, the 56x56 branch and the loss as a whole. The bottleneck attention is tested at full length (12b) but shows no gap-closure benefit, only a small fill-precision gain. They do not test the hole weight, the dilation rates or the number of transformer blocks. Statements in other sections about why the model works (for example, about the "two attention modules") must stay hedged ("we attribute this to", "is consistent with"). Do not strengthen them into causal claims ("shows that", "thanks to"), especially for the bottleneck attention.
14. The ablation run measured 98 ms per image for the full model, while the paper reports 91.6 ms. The difference comes from separate measurement runs. Do not add timings from the ablation run to the paper.
