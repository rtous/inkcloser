Highlights

- A mask-free network locates and closes gaps in line art for paint-bucket coloring
- Gated convolutions and two-scale self-attention let the network bridge long gaps
- A gap-focused loss combines hole-weighted cross-entropy with global and local Dice
- New metrics simulate the paint-bucket tool: gap closure, bucket accuracy and leaks
- Bucket accuracy rises from 64.1% to 94.7%, and 45% of drawings become leak-free
